## 2. What CityShield is

CityShield is a free app. It collects the outage and interruption notices published
for Varna and the surrounding area (water, power, heating, public transport) and
notifies you when a notice concerns your area.

**CityShield is not an official source.** We are not affiliated with the companies
whose notices we show, nor with Varna Municipality or any other public authority,
and we do not represent them. They have not approved the app and are not
responsible for it.

In an emergency call **112** or the company's emergency line. Do not rely on
CityShield alone.

## 3. Where the alerts come from

| Type | Who issues the notices | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Water | „Водоснабдяване и канализация – Варна" ООД (ViK Varna) | vikvarna.com |
| Power | „Електроразпределение Север" АД (ERP Sever) | erpsever.bg |
| Heating | Veolia Energy Varna | energy-varna.bg |
| Public transport | VarnaTraffic, run by map.bg, with data from „Градски транспорт" ЕАД | varnatraffic.com |

- The title and text of every alert are **word for word** what the company
  published. The text is theirs. We do not edit, check or confirm it.
- With every alert we name the company that issued it and link to the original
  notice.
- In the phone notification, long text may be shortened (marked with "…"). After the
  text we add, in brackets, the time of the interruption as read automatically from
  the notice.

## 4. How we decide who gets an alert

- Which places (settlements, districts, streets) and times a notice concerns is
  read from its text **automatically**, including with an AI model. The result is
  then compared with the location you set.
- This reading **can be wrong**. You may get an alert that does not concern you, or
  miss one that does. Points and areas on the map are approximate.
- For anything important, check the original notice (the link is in the alert) or
  call the company.
