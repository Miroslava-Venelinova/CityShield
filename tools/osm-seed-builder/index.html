OSM seed builder

Extract regions and streets from Overpass into the tool's own output/, generate seed.sql there and apply it to D1. backend/seeds/ is only touched by the explicit merge in step 4.

1 · Sweep a province

One input, the whole seed. Every city and village in the province, the districts inside each of them, and their streets — extracted in the three levels the app targets by: settlement → district → street. A district is found inside its settlement's own boundary, so which city it belongs to is not guessed afterwards. Everything lands in output/; nothing is applied yet.

Hundreds of Overpass queries in one go — use the self-hosted endpoint. A public mirror will rate-limit long before the end. Re-running is safe: the merge only ever adds, unless you tick the box above.

Regions: pick 4 to cover a whole province in one go — that is
          the scope the current regions seed was built at.

          
Streets: pick 8, one settlement at a time. A street row points
          at the settlement it is in (migration 0015), and a level 4 or 5 boundary spans
          hundreds of them, so there would be no single right answer to file the results
          under. It is more runs, but it is the only scope that carries the information
          the seed needs.

          
The Bulgaria extract also carries thin border strips of the neighbouring
          countries, which number their levels differently. Keeping the country field
          set to България is what keeps those out of your results.
