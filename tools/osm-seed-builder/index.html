LevelMeaningExample

8населено място — city, town, villageгр. Варна, с. Аврен

10квартал — neighbourhood (rare)Дебър, Любеново

Pick 4 to cover a whole province — that is the scope the current seed data was built at. Pick 8 for a single city.

The Bulgaria extract also carries thin border strips of the neighbouring countries, which number their levels differently. Keeping the country field set to България is what keeps those out of your results.

2 · What to extract

Drops Latin-script names — the extract includes border strips of the neighbouring countries, and alerts are written in Bulgarian.
Changing the country, area or kind regenerates the query and discards hand edits. Switching endpoint does not — it only changes where the query is sent.
